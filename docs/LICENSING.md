# Licensing FAQ

## Is it open source?

No. It is **source-available** under a dual license:

- academic/non-commercial: free under `LICENSE-ACADEMIC.md`;
- commercial: paid under `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`.

## What counts as academic?

Non-commercial research and teaching at an accredited non-profit institution,
with attribution. Company R&D, paid products, paid services, SaaS, and consulting
are commercial.

## What counts as commercial?

Any use that generates revenue or supports a commercial product/service,
including internal deployment at a for-profit company.

## Can I use it in a paper?

Yes, under the academic license, with attribution/citation. You must not claim
the algorithm as your own original work without citation.

## Can I sell a product built on it?

Only with a paid commercial license. Contact `2459551532@qq.com`.

## What about the NMF model?

`GUI/nmf_dict.npz` is derived from VoiceBank-DEMAND (CC BY 4.0). Attribution is
required; the CC BY 4.0 license applies to the model. A commercial code license
does not remove CC BY obligations.

## Can I contribute?

Yes, under `CLA.md`. This lets the author maintain the dual license.

## Is this legal advice?

No. Consult a lawyer for your jurisdiction and use case.
